## Visual Guidelines

### Colors
- Primary backgrounds: Obsidian Black (#080B10), Carbon Black (#111722)
- Primary actions: Racing Blue (#087BFF)
- Highlights: Electric Cyan (#21C8FF)
- Accents: Redline Red (#FF3048)
- Text: Clean White (#F5F7FA), Metallic Silver (#C7D0DC)

### Typography
- Headlines: Space Grotesk (bold, condensed)
- Body: Inter (clean, modern)
- Code/Terminal: IBM Plex Mono (monospace)

### Imagery
- Wet reflective floors
- Controlled blue lighting
- Carbon fiber and brushed metal
- Dark, dramatic, cinematic
- No bright or saturated colors
- No cartoon or illustration style
